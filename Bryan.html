Intersting Facts

Deepest Ocean Point

The Mariana Trench, located in the Pacific Ocean, is the deepest part of the world’s oceans, reaching about 36,070 feet (10,994 meters) at its lowest point, Challenger Deep. This trench is so deep that if Mount Everest were placed inside, its peak would still be underwater.

Octopus Heart & Blood

Octopuses are fascinating marine creatures with three hearts and blue-colored blood due to the presence of hemocyanin, a copper-based molecule that helps transport oxygen. Two of their hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third circulates it throughout the body. Interestingly, this third heart stops beating when they swim.

Banana vs. Berries

Botanically speaking, bananas are classified as berries, while strawberries are not. A berry is defined as a fleshy fruit containing seeds and developing from a single ovary. Bananas meet these criteria, whereas strawberries develop from multiple ovaries, making them an "aggregate fruit" rather than a true berry.
